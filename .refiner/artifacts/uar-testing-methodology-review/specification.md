# Specification — UAR testing-methodology review

- Artifact: `uar-testing-methodology-review`
- Type: `content` / `direct:content`
- Intent: verify that the public evidence taxonomy matches retained policy and
  does not turn diagnostic checks into certifying claims.

## Target state

1. Seven evidence classes state what they prove and do not prove.
2. Synthetic/recorded responses never certify genuine inference.
3. Fail-closed claims require observed negative controls and exact restoration.
4. Timing preserves Tier 0–3 and delivery-first broad-check boundaries.
5. Results remain bound to source SHA and named profile; Actions remain
   deployment-only.

## Limits

This is a documentation-source review. It runs no product test, model request,
soak, browser check, production build, or deployment.
