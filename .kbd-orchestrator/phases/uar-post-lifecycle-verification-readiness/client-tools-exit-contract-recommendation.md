## Retest and no-progress budget

Do not require the full suite twice after each test-only repair. Preserve already passing candidate-bound receipts when production bytes/configuration and the relevant fixture contract are unchanged. Rerun the failed mode plus any mode whose assertions/setup changed; after product edits, rerun affected shared behavior with a newly built candidate and retain what the old evidence cannot certify. A final consolidated pass is useful only if needed to establish one coherent candidate or if explicitly required by the adopted operator contract; it is not an automatic second full run.

Proposed finite budget: two failed repair/retest cycles for the same scenario without a newly observed causal discriminator, or60 minutes without a newly passing required scenario or a concrete narrowed defect. At either limit, stop that loop and give the operator the exact failure, source identity, minimal remaining blocker and a bounded choice. This is a stop-and-report budget, never permission to declare a failure nonblocking. Do not restart broad suites to manufacture apparent progress. Runtime wait limits already in the driver remain finite and must not be increased without a named observed reason.
