- FAIL: an executed product oracle is wrong. Preserve report and stop; discuss immediate correction versus backlog before any repair/retry.
- BLOCKED: missing fixture/authority/evidence, unknown or exhausted remaining allowance, unavailable build/environment, or unconfirmed effect. Record the exact missing prerequisite; do not label it a product PASS or assume an underlying product defect.
- NOT_EXERCISED: a required row never ran; it prevents successful closure.
- Neither FAIL nor BLOCKED closes this bound child as complete. Do not archive it, tick failed tasks complete, or run the normal child-exit helper. A separately approved pause/backlog handoff may reactivate the parent while leaving the child visibly incomplete. That is parking work, not successful exit. A scope reduction requires explicit amendment, retained failures and a narrower claim; it cannot rewrite history.
