# Independent design critique

Disposition: decision-complete for operator review of the proposed design. This is not implementation approval, gate opening, runtime PASS, or platform certification.

Scope: artifact-only reading of analysis.md, coverage-matrix.md, plan.md and exit-contract-draft.md. Native same-family critique is weaker than cross-model review. No machine sycophancy screen was run. No tests, checks, imports, builds, providers, services or product execution were invoked.

## Required design findings

No blocking design contradiction found in the four supplied artifacts. The design distinguishes approval of the proposal from adoption of the replacement gate policy and approval of actual execution. The existing closed gate therefore remains operative.

The design is complete enough to choose the architecture, but not to approve an executable sweep. The following are explicit decision dependencies already recognized by the proposal; they must remain visible when the operator considers it:

- **Budget feasibility is unresolved.** Analysis, “Limits and inheritance,” records 3909 of 4000 calls consumed; coverage-matrix.md, “Platform/backend applicability,” requires three positive journeys per acceptance cell and two fault journeys. Exit contract item 6 correctly requires scenario-derived totals and an amendment if necessary. With only 91 calls at the recorded snapshot, the complete proposed denominator has no demonstrated fit. Approval of this design must not imply approval of extra calls, time, repairs or either independent USD10 embedding allowance.
- **Applicability and semantic migration remain implementation work.** Plan step 1 requires mapping all 97 prior cases and 14 supporting obligations; the platform table leaves supported iOS persistence and any Windows restrictions dependent on documented design evidence. Exit contract item 3 forbids hiding missing cells. A registry must resolve these questions before the implementation-complete inventory; the current grouped matrix alone cannot prove retained coverage.
- **The exact policy replacement remains unadopted.** Analysis, “Policy redesign is explicit,” and plan steps 5–6 require explicit adoption plus exact execution approval. Their generated contract must reconcile the child-only repair allowance with the current rule that every later edit recloses the gate. An accepted architectural direction cannot itself authorize a correction, rebuild or rerun.

## Assessment against the requested simplification

The proposed dependency model actually removes the all-platform/all-store admission bottleneck: analysis, “Chosen architecture,” makes prerequisites local to cells, and plan, “User-first execution order,” starts with configured prompting. A serial runner does not prevent the team scenarios from measuring actual internal concurrency; coverage group G09 explicitly retains that observable requirement.

Coverage includes durable custom agents and protected default/orchestrator definitions (G03), routing selection events (G04), AG-UI/A2UI and protocol/client modes (G02), skill/context behavior (G05–G06), real KB regeneration and recovery (G07), both stores (G08), teams (G09), REST/startup maintenance (G10), and actual claimed platforms with Android deferred. The registry and migration map are necessary machinery; duplicating them into manually maintained authority documents would defeat the design.

The uncomfortable limitation is that a smaller runner cannot make unavailable Windows/iOS executors available or make the inherited budget sufficient. Those outcomes remain BLOCKED or require an explicit resource/scope decision, even if the local prompting path succeeds.

Unverified: completeness of production route discovery, legacy mappings, actual executor availability, numerical request/cost bounds, implementation behavior and all acceptance results. These are intentionally outside this planning critique.
