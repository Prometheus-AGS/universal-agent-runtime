## 0. Stop rule, recording and operator gates (before any code)

The-boss decision D3: agentic chunking is estimated at 3 days (upper bound) and stops at 4.5 recorded days without its gate passing. It shares an 8-day cap with `skill-matching-llm-and-local-embedding` (`Llm` 1 day, stop 1.5; `LocalEmbedding` 2 days, stop 3). Reaching the cap stops whichever items are still open. A stopped item is hidden from the-boss's settings and recorded as follow-up work. Effort is recorded in the same task log as the sibling change (child phase `the-boss-universal-agent-runtime`, proposed file `effort-log.md`, append-only); the phase's adversarial review checks it at each threshold.
