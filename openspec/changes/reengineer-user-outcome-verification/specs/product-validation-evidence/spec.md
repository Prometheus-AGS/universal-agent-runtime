## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Product acceptance measures useful user outcomes
UAR acceptance SHALL enumerate the supported user journeys and SHALL require a useful input-dependent model-produced result through each required mounted interface, with the configured provider/model and effective agent/policy identified. A successful HTTP status, valid error terminal, synthetic response, settings echo or build SHALL NOT independently establish a positive task outcome.

#### Scenario: Configured user completes a prompt
- **WHEN** a configured user submits a fresh fact transformation task through a supported interface
- **THEN** the actual configured model produces the expected task result with a successful protocol terminal and correlated agent/run evidence

#### Scenario: A fixture cannot exercise its required collaborator
- **WHEN** a required database, provider, embedding model or worker is unavailable
- **THEN** the affected acceptance case is blocked rather than passed, omitted or replaced with synthetic positive inference

### Requirement: Designed capability behavior is covered through durable user journeys
Acceptance SHALL cover persisted agents, effective skill and context strategies, complete request budgets, grounded knowledge and embedding-model regeneration, team threading and multi-call context, REST configuration and initial/warm startup maintenance. Required durable outcomes SHALL use the same outcome predicates on Postgres and durable SurrealDB, including true process restart. Every required case SHALL have fixed prerequisites, inputs, expected and failing outcomes, owner and redacted evidence before execution.

#### Scenario: Knowledge survives an embedding-model change
- **WHEN** a knowledge base with retained indexed source changes to a different embedding space and its required regeneration workflow is exercised
- **THEN** regenerated retrieval and grounded inference succeed without mixed-generation results or source loss, including the designed interrupted-rebuild recovery

#### Scenario: A selected strategy has no effective implementation
- **WHEN** a required selectable classifier or context strategy falls back or lacks its promised effect
- **THEN** the requested strategy is not certified by its configuration label and remains an explicit implementation or acceptance blocker

### Requirement: Acceptance preserves failures and bounded execution history
Acceptance SHALL preserve predecessor failures, actual consumption and unsupported dispositions, SHALL publish the exact case denominator and observed repetitions, and SHALL stop the approved serial packet at its first failed invocation or exhausted or unknown allowance. Raw redacted failure evidence SHALL be retained before exact-owned cleanup. Product outcome, publication and deployment health SHALL remain separate claims.

#### Scenario: Positive response is followed by a failed continuation
- **WHEN** a required multi-call or restart continuation fails after an earlier successful response
- **THEN** the journey is not passed and the failure remains in the evidence without an automatic retry replacing it

### Requirement: Strategy evidence records the actual governed implementation
Skill matching SHALL distinguish the default service engine from the selected intent-classifier engine. A selected model-dependent algorithm SHALL use its admitted model and eligible candidates or return an explicit error, without silently substituting keyword matching. Hierarchical context SHALL separately produce its designed summary and fact tiers when activated. Context preparation SHALL preserve protected tool groups and multimodal records or refuse protected overflow. Auxiliary requests to genuine providers MAY use explicitly approximate serialized-body counts with finite known-capacity allowances; those counts SHALL NOT establish an exact-fit guarantee.

#### Scenario: Semantic and lexical choices disagree
- **WHEN** a frozen low-overlap semantic vector and lexical control are submitted under their selected matching engines
- **THEN** acceptance records the actual algorithm, model, candidate scores and effective skill contribution rather than inferring selection from saved settings

#### Scenario: Genuine context summarization needs an auxiliary allowance
- **WHEN** a genuine model has a known capacity but no proven exact counting contract
- **THEN** its bounded auxiliary request records approximate counting quality and actual tier receipts, while the strict exact-contract checks remain unchanged

### Requirement: Knowledge regeneration has a durable cohort and explicit visibility
The owned regeneration endpoint SHALL bind a retained-document cohort to the active embedding-space fingerprint and persist generation progress. Old workers SHALL NOT publish into the replacement generation. Retrieval SHALL explicitly refuse an incomplete or incompatible generation. Both supported durable adapters SHALL preserve source bytes and support a changed vector dimension without mixing spaces. A failed generation SHALL remain visible until an explicit supported recovery action; acceptance SHALL NOT retry a failed gate automatically.

#### Scenario: Process stops during a replacement generation
- **WHEN** regeneration has durably admitted a cohort and the runtime is restarted before completion
- **THEN** the same retained cohort resumes under its target fingerprint, and only its completed coherent generation becomes searchable

### Requirement: Structured output claims match supported execution combinations
Direct supported completion requests SHALL forward the requested structured output contract to the genuine provider and validate the final result before presenting it as structured success. Unsupported graph-plus-structured combinations SHALL fail explicitly before model or tool effects rather than silently discard the format. Private classifier and summarizer calls SHALL retain their own contracts.

#### Scenario: A schema-only fresh nonce distinguishes enforcement
- **WHEN** a supported direct request includes a nonce only in its JSON schema and an owned genuine forwarding fixture records the actual provider format field
- **THEN** the final useful result satisfies that schema and the evidence correlates the unchanged upstream format with the run


### Requirement: Supported profiles preserve optional adapter boundaries
Transport-free host-persistence and embedded-mobile profiles SHALL keep shared execution-capability decisions available without selecting HTTP server adapters or a built-in UAR datastore. HTTP-only administration adapters SHALL compile only with the server feature. Concrete Surreal memory connections, subscriptions and reconnect controls SHALL compile only with the Surreal backend feature. A request to construct built-in memory without that backend SHALL explicitly refuse; it SHALL NOT invent a working backend or an engine-close acknowledgment. Existing enabled-backend lifecycle and server capability behavior SHALL be preserved.

#### Scenario: Rust SDK example admits its independent locked graph
- **WHEN** the approved shipped SDK example is built using its independent lockfile and transport-free runtime feature selection
- **THEN** the build does not require UAR's disabled HTTP or concrete Surreal adapter modules, and its result remains separate from runtime user-outcome acceptance

#### Scenario: Shared execution decisions serve server and embedded callers
- **WHEN** compiler or runtime code evaluates workflow and team capability decisions in a transport-free profile
- **THEN** it uses the shared domain contract with the same values and environment rules reexported by the enabled server API

#### Scenario: Built-in memory is requested without its optional backend
- **WHEN** a profile without the Surreal backend explicitly requests the built-in memory service or a backend-specific borrowed scope
- **THEN** construction refuses explicitly, no database is implicitly selected, and a missing engine cannot produce a successful close acknowledgment


### Requirement: Platform certification reports scoped runtime outcomes
This verification round SHALL prioritize certification of UAR's pinned Prometheus Parking Lot 0.4.0 integration before the broad functional sweep. Its platform scope SHALL be macOS, Linux, Windows and iOS. Android SHALL remain explicitly deferred with its historical failures and consumption preserved. A cross-build, source review or another operating system's runtime result SHALL NOT establish functional certification for a platform. Each platform SHALL identify its executed artifact, runtime host or device, supported capability applicability and observed outcomes. Missing executors SHALL remain explicit blockers.

#### Scenario: Android is deferred after a failed build
- **WHEN** the operator defers Android while selecting the four other platforms
- **THEN** the Android failure remains historical evidence, no Android PASS is inferred, and the new scope denominator is reported separately from the original sweep

#### Scenario: An iOS library compiles without a runtime journey
- **WHEN** the iOS cross-build passes but no configured prompt and supported persistence journey runs on its actual host integration
- **THEN** compilation is supporting evidence and iOS functional certification remains unverified

#### Scenario: Windows lacks an execution host
- **WHEN** no Windows executor has run the selected supported workflows
- **THEN** Windows remains blocked rather than inheriting a macOS or Linux functional result


### Requirement: Serial failure collection preserves ownership and prerequisites
An explicitly approved collection mode SHALL retain the complete case denominator, failed attempts and source/artifact identity. It SHALL permit at most one host-authorized targeted correction and one same-item rerun per failed item under the cumulative repair allowance. Missing or failed prerequisites SHALL block dependent items, while unrelated items MAY continue only after confirmed owned cleanup. The default first-failure mode SHALL remain available. All selected-platform runs SHALL retain one cumulative budget epoch without resetting provider reservations.

#### Scenario: A failed case cannot contaminate the next case
- **WHEN** a user-outcome case fails during collection
- **THEN** its private evidence is retained and its exact owned runtime/store cleanup must complete before independent dispatch; unknown cleanup stops the shared branch

#### Scenario: An upstream request outlives its canceled downstream waiter
- **WHEN** the fixture's downstream connection closes after a genuine request was dispatched
- **THEN** forwarding MAY drain that same upstream response under its original byte/time/reservation bounds and settle only actual observed usage; missing usage or upstream failure remains unknown without refund, retry or ledger reset

#### Scenario: The embedded SDK follows current conversation-policy contracts
- **WHEN** a host loads or deletes a conversation policy through the existing unscoped SDK API
- **THEN** it uses the same anonymous owner namespace as that API's record constructor, and effective configuration propagates the runtime's fallible result

### Requirement: Linux acceptance executes the attested Linux artifact
The Linux server path SHALL launch the extracted ELF from an immutable local image with source-bound preparation receipts, explicit owned network routes and mounts, and real container identity checks. A macOS controller or client SHALL NOT be described as a Linux server runtime. A foreground Docker client exit SHALL NOT establish runtime shutdown; the exact owned container must be stopped, joined and absent before datastore teardown.

#### Scenario: A dynamic negative peer needs an explicit Linux route
- **WHEN** a Linux negative-provider scenario is admitted
- **THEN** it uses the frozen owned listener port from preparation and the declared container-host route, preserving the actual host observer origin and denial predicate


#### Scenario: An iOS seed fails during approved collection
- **WHEN** a seed or restore invocation fails in the selected iOS repetition
- **THEN** its exact item identity, first attempt and private evidence remain retained; at most one actual host-authorized targeted correction and same-item rerun is admitted under the same cumulative epoch and repair allowance
- **AND** a restore whose seed remains unsuccessful is blocked while later independent repetitions may proceed only after confirmed owned process, private-input and datastore boundaries and settled provider accounting
- **AND** changed source or artifacts require explicit refreshed authority and identities; a changed live controller, unknown cleanup or exhausted allowance stops dispatch rather than granting a retry or a mixed-source PASS


#### Scenario: Canceled downstream leaves genuine forwarding pending
- **WHEN** a collection item has cleaned up its exact owned runtime and store but the shared proxy is still draining a previously dispatched upstream response
- **THEN** correction admission, same-item rerun and the next independent dispatch wait within the original epoch and remaining allowance for known settlement
- **AND** unknown accounting, source/owner mismatch or bounded settlement timeout blocks continuation while all initial failure receipts, full reservations and cumulative counts remain retained
- **AND** an explicitly observed terminal controlled upstream failure may settle without being relabeled useful success or causing another provider request

### Requirement: Preparation explicitly binds its child environment references
The frozen platform preparation configuration SHALL supply each named child environment reference through explicit literal values. Preparation SHALL preserve the pinned toolchain/cache selection and use its exact owned temporary scope. It SHALL NOT recover a missing binding by sourcing a shell profile or inheriting unrelated credentials.

#### Scenario: Artifact preparation requires the five base process variables
- **WHEN** the parent runs the native/SDK/gRPC artifact preparer with declared HOME, LANG, LC_ALL, PATH and TMPDIR references
- **THEN** the frozen parent artifact-stage environment explicitly supplies all five referenced UAR_PREP_BASE values, and the child receives only its declared selected variables


#### Scenario: iOS forwarding returns a known failed HTTP response
- **WHEN** the iOS fixture observes an internally consistent, fully received non-success upstream HTTP response whose reservation is terminal failed
- **THEN** the reservation may settle with its full accounting retained and the user item remains failed, allowing its authorized same-item correction or independent continuation only after confirmed cleanup
- **AND** pending requests wait only within existing bounds, while unknown, malformed or wrongly bound terminal evidence blocks continuation
- **AND** known settlement alone never establishes useful completion or an aggregate PASS for a failed model-produced result


### Requirement: Server and iOS terminal admission share evidence validation
Both acceptance consumers SHALL use one shared pure terminal-receipt validator for known completed/failed forwarding. It SHALL require binding/epoch/input/kind consistency, HTTP status/state agreement, finite positive chronologically ordered reservation/outbound/settlement timestamps, a well-formed response SHA256, bounded nonnegative response size, and actual successful usage within the caller's existing bound. Failed HTTP settlement SHALL retain its failed outcome and full reservation; unknown/malformed evidence SHALL block continuation. Ownership, cumulative accounting and time bounds SHALL remain enforced by their existing callers.

#### Scenario: A failed terminal receipt contains malformed evidence
- **WHEN** a terminal receipt has null, nonfinite or reversed timestamps, or a missing/malformed response digest
- **THEN** the same shared predicate refuses it in server and iOS admission before another dispatch, preserving all prior failures and reservations

#### Scenario: Terminal admission is consistent across both consumers
- **WHEN** valid known failed HTTP or successful model/embedding usage is evaluated under its existing caller limits
- **THEN** both consumers apply the shared validator without relaxing a stronger per-reservation bound or treating settlement as useful success


### Requirement: iOS settlement cannot succeed after its deadline
The iOS acceptance consumer SHALL pass the remaining settlement allowance into its read-only ledger snapshot, clip database lock/read waits to the remaining original and settlement bounds, close the read connection deterministically, and refuse late success after snapshot acquisition and immediately before returning settled evidence. Polling SHALL respect the remaining allowance.

#### Scenario: A snapshot completes after settlement or original expiry
- **WHEN** a SQLite read or terminal validation crosses either deadline, including an empty or fully completed snapshot
- **THEN** the consumer refuses settled success and admits no subsequent dispatch on that evidence
- **AND** existing reservations, failed outcomes, counters and original epoch are retained
