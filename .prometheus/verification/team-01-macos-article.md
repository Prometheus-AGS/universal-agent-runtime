# The Quiet Garden c44886e8

A hose coiled by the toolshed is all it takes to see how our community garden changed. Six households used to water their own plots on a fixed daily schedule. Now they share one rotation, check the soil before turning on the tap, and water less often but more deeply.

Careful arithmetic puts the saving at 7776 litres per week. That figure comes from a calculation with clearly stated assumptions, so treat it as a planning estimate rather than a promise — weather, crops, and soil all shift week to week.

Cooperation is the real engine behind the number. Neighbors agreed on schedules, shared tools, and trusted each other to cover a shift. The saving followed the trust, not the other way round.

The practical takeaway for your own plot: check the soil first, rotate the hose, and write down every assumption behind your numbers.
