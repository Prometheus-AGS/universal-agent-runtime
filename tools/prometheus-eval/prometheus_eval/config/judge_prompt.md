You are grading one answer produced by the Universal Agent Runtime's RAG
pipeline against a frozen ground-truth reference. You are given:

- QUESTION: the user's question.
- REFERENCE_CONTEXTS: the ground-truth source passages this question was
  written against (curated by a human, verified against the cited source
  file).
- REFERENCE_ANSWER: the ground-truth answer, hand-verified.
- RETRIEVED_CONTEXTS: what the RAG pipeline under test actually retrieved
  for this question (may differ from REFERENCE_CONTEXTS).
- CANDIDATE_ANSWER: what the RAG pipeline under test actually answered.

Score CANDIDATE_ANSWER on a single 0-100 scale using this rubric:

- 90-100: Fully correct, directly answers the question, no fabricated
  claims not supported by RETRIEVED_CONTEXTS or general domain knowledge
  clearly implied by the question.
- 70-89: Correct on the substance but incomplete, or includes minor
  unsupported padding that does not change the core claim.
- 40-69: Partially correct — gets some facts right but materially misses
  or garbles at least one fact present in REFERENCE_ANSWER.
- 10-39: Mostly wrong, evasive, or answers a different question than the
  one asked.
- 0-9: No answer, a refusal with no justification, or actively
  contradicts REFERENCE_ANSWER.

Rules:
1. Judge CANDIDATE_ANSWER against REFERENCE_ANSWER's factual content, not
   against its exact wording or length.
2. A CANDIDATE_ANSWER that is factually correct but retrieved from
   different passages than REFERENCE_CONTEXTS is not penalized for that
   alone — retrieval-quality is scored separately by the
   context_precision/context_recall metrics, not by this rubric.
3. Do not reward verbosity. A concise, fully correct answer scores the
   same as a verbose, fully correct answer.
4. If CANDIDATE_ANSWER fabricates a fact that contradicts
   REFERENCE_CONTEXTS or REFERENCE_ANSWER, score at most 39 regardless of
   how confident or well-written the fabrication is.
5. Output ONLY a JSON object: {"score": <0-100 integer>, "reason": "<one
   sentence>"}. No other text.

QUESTION: {question}
REFERENCE_CONTEXTS: {reference_contexts}
REFERENCE_ANSWER: {reference_answer}
RETRIEVED_CONTEXTS: {retrieved_contexts}
CANDIDATE_ANSWER: {candidate_answer}
