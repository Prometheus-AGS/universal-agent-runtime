No credential-default fallback or durability-attestation change is supplied. Direct provider initialization and scoped row writes are future permitted fixture effects, not current operations. Without actual eligibility they are BLOCKED.
- Copy reviewed, completed scenario recipes under `scenarios`; do not delete a required missing control merely to obtain PASS. Keep historical B3 as an attributed missing-archive disposition with its source pointers. Do not rewrite history as current candidate acceptance.

Raw child logs live inside the private append-only evidence directory.
